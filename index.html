About me

Turning data
into direction

Hi, I’m Bhavik Sathvara — a data analyst who turns complex data into clear reporting, forecasts and practical decisions.

I specialise in end-to-end analytics projects — from cleaning raw data and designing star-schema warehouses to machine learning forecasts, computer vision models and interactive dashboards in Power BI, Excel and Streamlit.

My work spans healthcare, software, technology, retail supply chain and security operations. I hold an MSc in Information Systems from the University of West London, where my dissertation built a real-time helmet detection system using YOLO object detection.
